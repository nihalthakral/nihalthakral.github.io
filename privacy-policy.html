Privacy Policy for Goat

Last updated: August 10, 2026
Goat does not collect, store, or transmit any of your data to us or to any third party. Everything the app reads stays on your device, in memory, for as long as the app is open.

1. What Goat Does

Goat is a simple, on-device utility that shows you a chronological history of when apps on your phone were opened. It reads this information directly from Android's built-in Usage Access system and displays it to you, for a range of today up to the last 7 days.

2. Permissions We Request

Goat asks for a single permission:

  • Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS) — used to read the timestamps of when apps were opened on your device, so Goat can build the history timeline shown in the app. This is an Android system permission you grant manually from Settings, and it can be revoked by you at any time.

Goat does not request access to your contacts, messages, location, camera, microphone, photos, files, or any other data on your device.

3. Data Collection and Storage

  • Goat does not have internet or network access — it cannot send data anywhere, even if it wanted to.
  • Goat does not use analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs.
  • Goat does not create accounts, logins, or any form of user identity.
  • App usage history is read fresh each time you open Goat and is never written to a database, file, or backup.
  • Closing the app clears everything from memory — nothing persists between sessions on our end, because nothing ever leaves your device in the first place.

4. Data Sharing

Since Goat never collects or transmits any data, there is nothing for us to share, sell, or disclose to third parties — including advertisers, analytics providers, or law enforcement, as we do not hold any user data to begin with.

5. Children's Privacy

Goat does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13, because it does not collect information from anyone at all.

6. Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of Goat adds a feature that requires new permissions — this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect that change.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how Goat works, you can reach out via the app's listing page on the Play Store.
