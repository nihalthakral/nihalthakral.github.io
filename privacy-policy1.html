Goat: Anti-Scam & Fraud Shield

Effective date: 10 August 2026  •  Package name: com.goat.app  •  Last updated: 10 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Goat: Anti-Scam & Fraud Shield Android application ("Goat", "the App", "we", "us") handles information when you install and use the App. Goat is designed around a simple principle: your personal activity data stays on your device. We do not operate user accounts, we do not run analytics or advertising SDKs inside the App, and we do not transmit your app-usage history to any server.

1. What Goat Does

Goat helps you avoid scams and fraud by watching for a specific moment: when you open an app you've chosen to protect (for example, a banking, payments, or messaging app) after coming from your home screen. At that moment, Goat can show a brief on-screen safety reminder before you continue, and it can show you which of your installed apps look safe versus which show signals worth double-checking. Goat also keeps a short, local, on-device log of when your selected apps were opened, so you can review it yourself.

2. Information We Access and Why

All of the information below is processed locally on your device. It is not uploaded to us or to any third party.

Data / Permission Why Goat needs it Leaves your device?
List of installed apps QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES To show you the list of apps on your device so you can choose which ones to protect, and to check each app's basic signals (e.g. install source, permissions it holds) to flag possible risk. No
App usage / open events PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS To detect the moment a protected app is opened (so a safety reminder can be shown), and to build the local "Phone History" screen showing recent app-open times, entirely on your device. No

Foreground service FOREGROUND_SERVICE To keep the monitoring feature running reliably while your screen is on, with a persistent notification so you always know it's active. No
Run at device startup RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED To resume monitoring after you restart your phone, without you having to reopen the App. No
Ignore battery optimizations To reduce the chance Android puts the monitoring service to sleep, so protection keeps working reliably. No
In plain terms: Goat reads which apps you have and when you open the ones you chose to monitor, purely to run the on-device safety-check feature and show you your own history. This information is stored in the App's local storage on your phone and is not sent to Goat's developer or to any advertiser.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

  • Monitored/excluded app list — your choices about which apps to protect, saved in the App's local preferences.
  • App-open history — timestamps and app names for recent opens of your monitored apps, used only to power the in-app "Phone History" screen. This is read live from Android's system usage stats and is not copied elsewhere.
  • Optional debug logs — if you use the App's debugging/troubleshooting option, a plain-text log file may be saved to your device's Downloads folder. This only happens if you trigger it, stays on your device, and is meant to help you (or a support contact you choose to send it to) diagnose issues.

Uninstalling the App removes this locally stored data from your device.

4. Network Access

The App makes a limited network request to fetch a small, static, publicly hosted list of trusted app-store package names, used to help judge whether an app was installed from a recognized store. This request:

  • Does not include your app list, usage history, device identifiers, or any personal data;
  • Is a simple read-only fetch of a public JSON file;
  • Is the only outbound network connection the App makes.

Goat does not include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or crash-reporting SDKs that transmit usage data off your device.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. Since app-usage and app-list data never leave your device, there is nothing for us to share — we simply don't receive it.

6. Your Choices & Controls

  • Choose what's monitored: you can select or deselect individual apps for protection at any time in Settings.
  • Revoke permissions: you can disable Usage Access, Display-over-other-apps, or other permissions any time via Android Settings; doing so will limit or stop the corresponding feature.
  • Clear history: the in-app history reflects Android's own usage-stats records; clearing the App's storage/data from Android Settings removes Goat's local preferences.
  • Uninstall: removing the App deletes its local data and stops all monitoring.

7. Children's Privacy

Goat is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the App does not collect or transmit personal data to us in the first place, no such data about any user, including children, is retained on our servers.

8. Data Security

Because sensitive activity data is processed and stored locally using Android's standard app-private storage, it is protected by your device's own security (lock screen, encryption, OS sandboxing). We recommend keeping your device's operating system up to date and using a secure lock method.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if the App's features change. We will update the "Last updated" date above when changes are made. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
