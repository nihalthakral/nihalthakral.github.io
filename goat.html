About this app
GOAT – Stay One Step Ahead of Scams In a world where scams are getting smarter every day, Goat helps you stay alert exactly when it matters most — right before you open the apps that matter, like your banking, payment, or shopping apps. How it works: Choose the apps you want Goat to watch over — your banking app, UPI/payment apps, or any app where a scam could cost you real money. Every time you open one of these apps, Goat instantly shows a quick, non-intrusive safety reminder on your screen, giving you a moment to pause and think before you proceed. No complicated setup, no constant notifications — just a simple check exactly when you need it. Why Goat? 🛡️ Instant Safety Reminders – A quick on-screen check appears the moment you open a protected app, helping you stay alert to common scam tactics. 🎯 You Choose What's Protected – Pick exactly which apps trigger the safety check. Full control, zero clutter. ⚡ Lightweight & Fast – Runs quietly in the background without slowing down your phone or draining your battery. 🔒 Privacy-Friendly – Goat doesn't read your messages, calls, or personal data. It simply watches for when a protected app opens — nothing more. 📱 Simple, Clean Design – No confusing menus. Set it up once in under a minute and forget about it. Who is Goat for? Anyone who uses banking, UPI, or payment apps on their phone and wants an extra layer of awareness against scams, phishing attempts, and social engineering tricks — especially useful for first-time smartphone users, parents setting up phones for family members, or anyone who wants a simple safety net. Goat is free to use and supported by minimal, non-intrusive ads — so you get protection without paying a rupee. Stay sharp. Stay safe. Let Goat watch your back.
